## Description

Meet DeskBuddies, a lightweight collection of animated desktop pets for your Mac. Choose a cat, dog, panda, or sloth and keep a cheerful companion on your desktop while you work.

Your buddy reacts when you click, poke, scratch, or drag it. In Walk Around mode, it explores a limited section of your screen, pauses to groom, and changes its pace as your Mac's thermal state changes.

Features:

* Four animated pet profiles: cat, dog, panda, and sloth
* Stay Put and Walk Around modes
* Click, poke, scratch, drag, purr, meow, and playful reactions
* Adjustable pet size and optional Always on Top behavior
* Thermal-state expressions using Apple's supported ProcessInfo API
* Menu-bar controls and a compact settings window
* Completely offline with no analytics, accounts, ads, or tracking

DeskBuddies reports only Apple's general thermal categories. It does not read private sensors or display an exact temperature.

## App Review Notes

DeskBuddies is a menu-bar macOS desktop-pet app. When launched, it creates a transparent, borderless pet window rather than a conventional document window. The menu-bar pet icon opens all controls and Settings.

To test interaction, click or repeatedly poke the pet for different expressions and sounds, move the pointer back and forth over it to trigger scratching and relaxation, or drag the pet to reposition it. Enable Movement > Walk Around to test autonomous walking and grooming. Always on Top changes the transparent window level.

Settings includes manual Cool, Warm, Hot, and Critical previews so review can verify every visual state without heating the Mac. System mode uses only Apple's public `ProcessInfo.processInfo.thermalState` API. The app has no SMC access, private sensor access, network client, analytics, or user accounts.
