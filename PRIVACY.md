# DeskBuddies Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

DeskBuddies does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app works completely offline. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, update checker, or network client. Pet preferences and window position are stored locally on the user's Mac through standard macOS preferences.

DeskBuddies reads the Mac's current thermal category using Apple's supported `ProcessInfo.processInfo.thermalState` API. This provides only a system state such as nominal, fair, serious, or critical. DeskBuddies does not access the System Management Controller, private sensor data, or exact temperature readings.

DeskBuddies does not access files, contacts, calendars, location, photos, microphone, camera, or other personal content.

Questions or support requests can be submitted through the [DeskBuddies support page](https://github.com/vinayanand3/DeskBuddies/issues).
